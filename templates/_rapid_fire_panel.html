Settings

Take-profit plan: waiting for the instrument data.

Enter the money you want to make per fire; TradeMate reads the instrument's tick size and tick value from MT5, uses your lot and works out the take-profit distance and price itself. The spread is already inside that distance (a buy fills at the ask and exits on the bid), the result is rounded up to a whole tick, and commission is only included if you enter it. If the broker data is missing or invalid nothing is guessed and no order is sent. The exit is a software-managed level (no broker-side TP).

The emergency exit is how Rapid Fire fits the Risk Engine without a broker stop-loss: it is a software level, so it only works while TradeMate, the bridge and the MT5 terminal are running. Required before Start.

Anti-Twin

ON or OFF, nothing else to set. When a position has moved away from its entry price (in profit or against you) and price then returns to, or crosses, that entry price, it is closed, the closure is confirmed, and an equal-volume opposite position opens. Each new position is watched from its own entry price, so BUY, SELL, BUY, SELL can continue (up to a fixed safety ceiling). A BUY is judged on the bid and a SELL on the ask, so the spread alone never counts as a crossing. The emergency exit and the Emergency Stop always win. This switch is independent of Candle Momentum and Bull/Bear.

Lot helper

Uses the broker's tick value for the instrument. It never assumes what a point is worth and does not convert currencies.
