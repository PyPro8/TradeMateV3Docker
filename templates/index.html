Settings for this instrument and timeframe

Anti-Twin reversal (optional, off by default)

ON or OFF, nothing else to set. As soon as the trade's price moves against it (its exit price becomes one tick worse than where the trade started), the trade is closed, the closure is confirmed, and an equal-volume opposite trade opens straight away. The new trade is watched the same way from its own entry price, so the switching can repeat (up to a fixed safety ceiling). It does not wait for a profit, a loss size, a candle close or the take-profit. A take-profit, a stop-loss or the Emergency Stop never triggers a reversal, and the Emergency Stop always wins.

Settings cannot change while Autopilot is running. Distances are converted by TradeMate’s instrument engine, never by a fixed forex pip.

        Supported operations

        TradeMate reads the identity of the account already logged in to the configured MT5 terminal. The displayed login, server and environment come from the fresh broker snapshot, not a locally saved account label.

        This bridge supports one active terminal connection and has no account-login or account-switch command. TradeMate therefore cannot safely save or switch account profiles. Do not change the MT5 login while TradeMate commands are pending or unresolved: bridge commands are not pinned to an account login. After the terminal reconnects, this page reflects only the newly verified snapshot.

        Credentials are neither requested nor stored by TradeMate. Real accounts are rejected by this MT5 adapter; supported execution remains DEMO-only through the existing Risk and Execution Engines.

        Risk and automation

        Risk limits are stored in TradeMate's local settings and currently apply to the connected account as a single configuration; per-account profiles and permissions are not implemented.

        Command Centre analysis is read-only. Manual orders use the existing server-side Risk Engine and DEMO-only Execution Engine. Autopilot (Trading controls above) only executes setups that your strategy produces and the Risk Engine approves.
